### CM-01 — Kinetic Typography

- Source/author: MotionElements / Alexhive. [Item 11451102](https://www.motionelements.com/after-effects-template-11451102-kinetic-typography).
- Observed: poster has centered condensed, stacked type over an architectural circle. Advancing preview at 20.65s shows a street signal; 29.60s shows a five-line red/white type stack over a performer; paused at 39.70s, the central stack ends in a substantially larger “UP”. Watermark remained visible.
- Useful principle: type scale and a restricted accent hierarchy can focus busy footage; the foreground message must remain legible as the background changes. Adapt grouping/scale, not the wording, condensed face or exact stack. Verify holds against actual subject occlusion and reading load.
- Limitation: sampled states do not establish precise entrance curves or duration. Busy subjects can compete with centered type. Listing says CS6+ and no required plugins; runtime, editability and included-asset rights were not tested.

### CM-04 — Sport Opener
